Before You Arrive

As this is a compact four-day workshop, participants are asked to complete some pre-work in advance to make full use of in-person time:

  1. Review the pre-workshop guide (sent to confirmed participants)
  2. Install and test Amua on your laptop
  3. Come prepared with a draft policy question for your country's policy brief
  4. Gather any available secondary data on your country's status quo strategy

What to Bring

Required Software

  • Laptop with Microsoft Excel installed
  • Amua (open-source decision modeling software) pre-installed and tested

Documents

  • Passport (valid for travel and visa requirements)
  • Draft policy question and any secondary data for your country brief

Other Essentials

  • Laptop power adapter
  • Notebook and pens
