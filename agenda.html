Workshop Agenda

December 7–10, 2026 | Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

Overview

The four-day Regional Health Economics TOT builds participants' skills in cost-effectiveness analysis, decision tree modeling in Excel and Amua, and mentoring future D2P trainees on economic evaluation. Each country group works throughout the week on a pre-determined policy brief, with real-time feedback from trainers on data sources and analysis. The curriculum below is a draft and is subject to change prior to the workshop.
