Workshop Resources

This page will host presentations, exercises, and reference guides for the Regional Health Economics TOT as they are finalized. Because the workshop is still in the participant selection stage, materials, group assignments, and country policy brief documents will be added once participants are confirmed.

Note: Confirmed participants will receive direct links to lecture slides, templates, and group materials by email ahead of the workshop. This page will be updated as those materials are finalized.

Pre-Workshop Preparation

🧩 Before You Arrive

As this is a compact four-day workshop, participants are asked to complete some pre-work in advance to make full use of in-person time.

  • Review the pre-workshop guide (to be distributed after confirmation)
  • Install and test Amua on your laptop before arrival
  • Come prepared with a draft policy question for your country's policy brief
  • Gather any available secondary data on your country's status quo strategy

Core Curriculum Topics

📊 Lecture Sessions

Presentations covering the economic evaluation curriculum for the TOT

  • Closer Look: Economic Modules / Framing an Economic Evaluation
  • Decision Trees
  • Common Errors and Challenges in D2P Decision Trees
  • Cost Analysis
  • Literature Search: Finding Epidemiological and Cost Data
  • Introduction to Amua
  • Health Outcomes in CEA: QALYs, DALYs, and Treatment Thresholds
  • Using AI in Decision Modeling and Economic Evaluation
  • Economic Evaluation Methods and ICER
  • Sensitivity Analysis

✏️ Hands-On Group Work

Each country group will build out a decision tree model for a pre-determined country policy brief, with trainer feedback throughout the week.

  • Setting Up the Status Quo Decision Tree (Excel)
  • Working on the Status Quo Model (Amua)
  • Creating a Policy Intervention (Amua)
  • Troubleshooting Interventions
  • Interpretation of Results and ICER Exercise

Technical Requirements

Required Software

  • Microsoft Excel (or compatible)
  • Amua (open-source decision modeling software)
  • Internet browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Safari)

What to Bring

  • Laptop with Excel and Amua pre-installed
  • Power adapter
  • Any available secondary data for your country's status quo strategy
